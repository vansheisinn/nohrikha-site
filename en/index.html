01 / About

Preserve memories.
Discover connections.

This project begins with a search. We want to understand what people remember about Nohrikha, compare sources and gradually reconstruct her story — with care for people and accuracy about evidence.

  1. 01

    Find people

    Hear from people who recognise the word “Nohrikha” and understand the context in which they heard it.

  2. 02

    Gather sources

    Find family memories, letters, photographs and documents that may help the research.

  3. 03

    Verify information

    Distinguish memories and assumptions from confirmed information, and document the origins of materials.
