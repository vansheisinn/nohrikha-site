From the search story
How it began

One name, carried through generations

My grandfather, born in 1938 to a family of Volga Germans, often said: “We should go to Babka Nohrikha.” He used the name when someone needed healing, advice, or an answer nobody else could give.

Over time, I realized everyone remembered the expression but no one could explain where it came from. Years of searching brought me to a literary work using the same name—and eventually to another family that remembered the woman herself.
