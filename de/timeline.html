A family timeline

Timeline

What the families remembered, arranged in time. Some dates are approximate; Nohrikha’s birth and death remain unknown.

Colours distinguish the family accounts. Dotted lines mark gaps in what is known.

NohrikhaFamily from China and KazakhstanMy grandfather’s family

This chart does not imply that my grandfather met Nohrikha personally. How he heard of her remains unknown.

Before China

Nohrikha was remembered as living in Bolshaya Bukon, Kazakhstan, before leaving for China. The departure date is unknown.

1939

My grandfather was born. He would later often say the name “Babka Nohrikha”.

1941

His German relatives were deported from the Volga region and subsequently lived in the Urals.

1942

A woman whose family remembered Nohrikha was born in China.

c. 1946–1950

According to family history, some of my grandfather’s relatives moved from Kazakhstan to the Urals.

c. 1954

The other family returned from China to Kazakhstan. Nohrikha apparently remained in Bolshaya Bukon, while they settled in Beskaragay, Pavlodar Region.

1970s

Most of the other family moved from Pavlodar Region to Latvia.

2005–2021

Online searches for the name, followed by the discovery of a play mentioning Babka Nohrikha.

2026

Correspondence brought a new family account of the woman known as Babka Nohrikha.

What we still hope to learn

Nohrikha’s first name, the dates of her life, the details of her travels—and how her memory reached my grandfather’s family.
