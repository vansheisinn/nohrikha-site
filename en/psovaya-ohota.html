Family accounts

A Name I Remember from ChildhoodZhanna Drozdovskaya’s family

Psovaya okhota is a dramatic work by Zhanna Drozdovskaya about a family across generations, separation, memory and the search for home. In one scene, a character mentions Granny Nohrikha, a woman remembered in the author's family.

That mention helped two family stories find each other years later.
